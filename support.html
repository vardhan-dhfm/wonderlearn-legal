WonderLearn help & support

WonderLearn is the learning app your institute gives you: video lessons, challenges, points, badges and leaderboards.

Signing in

Your account is created by your institute. Sign in with the username, phone number or email and the password your institute gave you, or ask for a one-time code. If you can't sign in, ask your teacher or your institute office. There is no separate sign-up.

A lesson or challenge is locked

Lessons open as you learn: finish earlier lessons and earn points to unlock the next ones. Your institute decides which lessons and challenges are open for you.

Strict challenges and the camera

Some challenges use the front camera to check that you are the one taking them. If the camera doesn't start, open your phone's Settings › WonderLearn and turn on Camera. On a phone with Screen Time or parental controls, ask a parent or guardian to allow the camera.

A video doesn't play

Check your internet connection and try again. If one video never plays, tell your teacher.

Report a nickname

On the leaderboard, touch and hold a name that isn't kind and choose Report name. We check it and change it if needed.

Delete your account

In the app open Me › Delete my account, or email us with your sign-in and your institute's name. It is done within 30 days.

Contact us

Email admin@snap.pe. We usually reply within 2 working days.
