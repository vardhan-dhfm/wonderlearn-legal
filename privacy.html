WonderLearn Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

WonderLearn is a learning app that institutes (schools, coaching centres and other learning institutes) give to their learners. It is provided by Divigo India Private Limited ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Who decides about your information

Your account is created by your institute. Your institute decides which lessons and challenges you see, and it can see your progress and results. We look after the information on the institute's behalf and only use it to run WonderLearn for you and your institute.

Information we use

  • Account details from your institute: your name, the username, phone number or email you sign in with, and which institute you belong to.
  • Your profile: the nickname and avatar you choose. Other learners see only your nickname, avatar, level and points on leaderboards, never your real name.
  • Learning activity: lessons watched, challenge answers and scores, points, badges, streaks and certificates.
  • Strict challenge records (only when your institute turns on strict rules for a challenge, see below).
  • Technical details needed to keep you signed in safely, such as a sign-in token stored securely on your phone.

We do not use your location, contacts or microphone. We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your information.

Strict challenges and the camera

Some challenges have strict rules, turned on by your institute. Before such a challenge starts, the app explains the rules and asks you to agree. During a strict challenge only:

  • The front camera checks, on your phone, that your face is in view and that it is still you. This face check runs entirely on the phone. The face measurements it uses are never sent to us, and are deleted from the phone when the challenge ends.
  • A few photos are taken: one at the start, one when a problem is seen (for example no face, or more than one person), and one every few minutes. These photos are sent to your institute so your teacher can review the challenge.
  • The app records if you leave the app, take a screenshot or record the screen during the challenge, and tells your institute.

The camera is never used outside strict challenges. You can turn camera access off at any time in your phone's Settings; strict challenges then can't be taken, but everything else in the app still works.

Children

Many WonderLearn learners are children. Their accounts are created and managed by their institute, which is responsible for getting a parent's or guardian's permission where the law requires it. Nicknames keep real names private, and a learner appears on the leaderboard for learners outside their institute only when their institute allows it (and, where the institute requires it, after it has recorded a parent's permission). Learners can hide themselves from leaderboards in Me.

Videos from other services

Some lessons play videos from YouTube (in its privacy-enhanced mode) or Google Drive inside the app. When such a video plays, that service receives the technical information it needs to stream it (such as your device's IP address), under its own privacy policy. We do not share your account details with these services.

Who can see your information

  • Your institute and its teachers: your progress, results and strict challenge records.
  • Other learners: only your nickname, avatar, level and points on leaderboards (if you are visible there).
  • Service providers who host our servers for us, bound to keep the information safe and use it only for WonderLearn.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active with your institute. When the account is deleted, your information is deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it longer.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Me › Delete my account to send a deletion request, or email us at the address below with your sign-in and your institute's name. We delete the account and its data (progress, points, results and challenge photos) within 30 days and let your institute know.

Keeping it safe

All information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and your sign-in is stored in your phone's secure storage.

Your choices and rights

You (or your parent or guardian) can ask to see, correct or delete your information by contacting your institute or us. Reports about another learner's nickname can be made from the leaderboard by touching and holding the name.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
